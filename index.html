NASA Glenn · Acoustics · Experiment

Noise-reducing acoustic filters

We designed filters to cut noise pollution and tested them in an acoustic tube. The speaker and microphone sit inside the tube, so every filter faces the same sound source and the same mic position, and the readings can be compared fairly.

  1. Built a small amplifier circuit to drive the speaker.
  2. Designed filters in Tinkercad, like the honeycomb-hole version above, then printed and tested them.
  3. Measured the sound level in decibels with and without each filter.

Why it matters: decibels are logarithmic, so a 10 dB drop means about 90% less sound energy.

Mechanisms · Iteration

Carnival bean-bag launcher

Our task was to build a carnival game. We started with a shooting-style game and a rubber-band launcher, but the prototype wasn't strong or consistent enough.

  1. Prototype: rubber bands on a metal frame. Too weak, and the launches varied.
  2. Diagnosis: rubber bands lose energy as heat and weaken with every use.
  3. Redesign: a larger frame with steel springs, which return almost all their stored energy and pull with the same force every time.
  4. Result: the bean bag launched farther and more consistently.
